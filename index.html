KILLER ACCESSIBILITY USP

The Virtual Reticle:
Zero-Occlusion Finger Drawing

Drawing with a finger on a smartphone has always suffered from the classic "fat-finger occlusion problem": your fingertip covers the exact point you are trying to illustrate.

Fluid Vector AR solves this natively with an adjustable Virtual Reticle Pointer. Touch anywhere, and the active stroke is drawn at a calibrated crosshair offset above your touch point. 100% visibility. Zero stylus required.
